Evaluator Guide & Task Prompts

Emergency Scenario

You are commuting/walking alone at night along a dimly lit route near USTP / Canitoan, Cagayan de Oro City. You need discreet help without pulling out a bright phone or sounding a loud alarm. The coin-scale remote in your pocket can silently dispatch a distress alert with live GPS and start encrypted background audio evidence recording — as long as it is paired to your iPhone over Bluetooth Low Energy.

Step-by-Step Task Prompts

  1. The iPhone starts screen OFF. Press its physical Side button (right edge) to wake to the Lockscreen, then swipe up from the lower half to unlock. Swipe down from the top-left for Notification Center, or from the top-right for Control Center.
  2. Press the remote's Blue side button to power it ON. Observe the top pinhole LED pulsing Blue — Standby / Searching. Notice the iPhone is not yet connected.
  3. On the iPhone, manually turn ON Bluetooth via Control Center (top-right swipe) or Settings → Bluetooth. Within ~0.6 s the top LED becomes solid Blue, the Dynamic Island confirms the connection, and a frosted notification appears.
  4. Use the iPhone's physical Side button to turn the screen OFF (or leave it on the Lockscreen). Press the recessed Magenta center button on the remote. The iPhone wakes silently, the top LED turns Green, the recording pill timer ticks upward, two rich notifications appear, and the 3 contacts transition Sending… → Delivered ✓.
  5. Press the Magenta center button again to stop/cancel: the timer stops, the clip is saved to Voice Memos, the LED returns to solid Blue, and an "Alert Ended" notification appears — no power cycle needed. Also test accidental-press protection (center press while remote is OFF is ignored), then press the Blue side button to power the remote OFF.

Simulated vs. Implemented

Simulated (no real hardware/radio)

  • BLE radio discovery & pairing handshake (timed state transition, no actual Bluetooth radio).
  • SMS / push delivery to external trusted contacts (UI status animation only).
  • Background encrypted audio capture (visual waveform; microphone is not accessed).
  • iOS Control Center, Dynamic Island, notifications and app navigation are faithful mockups.

Interactively Implemented

  • Two-button remote state machine (OFF → SEARCHING → CONNECTED → EMERGENCY).
  • Manual iPhone Bluetooth toggle (Control Center + Settings) that gates pairing.
  • Screen OFF / Lockscreen / Home Screen navigation with physical side button and swipe-up unlock.
  • Swipe-down Notification Center and Control Center with manual Bluetooth toggle.
  • Rich notification stack, live ticking MM:SS recording timer, contact dispatch animation.
  • Center-button stop/cancel toggle that saves the clip and returns to Blue standby.
  • GPS lookup via navigator.geolocation with CDO fallback, and ToT stopwatch.
  • Event log with timestamps and optional haptic feedback on mobile.
